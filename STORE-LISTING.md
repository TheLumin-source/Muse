# Muse

**A visual board for your ideas that lives on your own PC.**

Muse is a place to think with your eyes. Drop notes, images, links, files, to-do
lists and code onto an endless canvas and arrange them until the idea makes
sense. It works like the mood-board and planning tools you already know, with
one big difference: everything stays on your computer. No account, no monthly
fee, no uploading your work to someone else's cloud.

---

## Why you'll like it

- **Everything in one place.** Notes, images, web links, PDFs, to-do lists,
  colour swatches and code, all on one canvas you can pan and zoom around.
- **Boards inside boards.** Group work into boards with their own icon, and open
  them up like folders. Perfect for keeping a big project tidy.
- **Start fast with templates.** 26 ready-made boards for mood boards, brand and
  logo work, game design documents, storyboards, project plans and more.
- **Your files, kept live.** Point a card at a real file or a folder on your PC.
  When a new render or export lands in that folder, it shows up on your board by
  itself.
- **Grab anything in a keystroke.** Press the capture shortcut from any app,
  drag a box around what you want, and it pins straight onto your board.
- **Light or dark, your call.** A warm, calm workspace either way.
- **Present and share.** Step through a board full screen for a client, or export
  a board as a **PDF, an image, or a single web page** you can email to anyone.
- **Real previews.** Drop in a PDF and see its first page, not just an icon.
  Click any image to view it full size. Copy any image or file straight back out
  to Photoshop, Explorer or an email.
- **It's yours.** Boards are saved as plain files in your Documents folder. Back
  them up, move them to another PC, or open them in ten years. They are not
  locked inside anything.

## Made for

Designers, illustrators, game developers, writers, students, and anyone who
plans and gathers reference visually.

## What you get

- The Muse app for Windows
- 26 starter templates
- Free updates (Muse checks for new versions and updates itself when you say so)
- A one-time purchase. No subscription.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
- About 250 MB of free space
- Microsoft Edge WebView2, which is already on virtually every up-to-date
  Windows PC. If it is missing, Windows installs it automatically the first time
  you open Muse.

## Installing

1. Download **Muse Setup.exe** from your order.
2. Double-click it and follow the short installer. It installs just for you, so
   there is no admin password to enter.
3. Open Muse from the desktop shortcut or the Start menu.

Your boards are saved automatically in **Documents\Muse**. To move Muse to a new
PC, install it there and copy that folder across.

## Privacy

Muse keeps your work on your machine. It only reaches the internet to check for
an update, and to fetch a preview image when you paste a web link or add a live
file. Nothing else leaves your PC.
